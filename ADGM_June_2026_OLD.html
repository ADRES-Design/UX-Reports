Recommendations & Opportunities

Prioritised actions linked to the biggest pain points, with the share of tickets each would address.

P0 — Critical

Make the platform reliable at the point of transaction

Fix AccessRP registration/renewal and premise-ID validation errors with clear reference codes; add post-maintenance testing; clear stale "expired licence" states; treat Madhmoun permit failures as a dedicated incident; stabilise login and account-linking.

▸ Tackles the largest and fastest-growing problem (system errors). 2135321381
P0 — High ROI

Let people help themselves (self-service jumped in June)

In-platform editing of licence and profile details (instant for low-risk fields, verified upload for ID/nationality/legal name); document replace; self-service report and unit-list export.

▸ Removes ~20% of tickets that are pure manual handling. 212882132021298
P1 — High

Show the rules before users start

Put business rules on the service page up-front (auto-closure, expiry, early-termination penalties, the 5% rent cap); add fee breakdowns and tax invoices; contextual help on warnings; proactive expiry notifications.

▸ Deflects much of the ~29% rules/fees load. 213112132421296
P1 — Targeted

Track approvals and show status

Approval tracker with notifications to second parties/owners/banks; separate ADGM’s own review time from third-party waits and publish status; fast-track high-value transfers and mortgage releases.

▸ Improves ~18% of tickets, high value per case. 213152130921357
