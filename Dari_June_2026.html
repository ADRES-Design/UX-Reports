5. Recurring User Pain Points

The recurring patterns behind the frustration — each with the behaviour, why it happens, and the fix. (Helpdesk noise is filtered at intake, so four active pain patterns remain.)

Self-Service Gaps

Behaviour: Users email support to update identity, company details, create accounts, or cancel applications.

Why it happens: No in-platform self-service editor or cancellation flow exists — support becomes the missing UI.

What would fix it: Profile/identity editor (instant + verified tracks); company-detail updates; self-serve account creation and cancellation.

204790204143204289
Unclear Errors

Behaviour: Actions fail server-side or throw generic "Something went wrong" messages with no reference code.

Why it happens: Errors are non-diagnostic and unrecoverable; users cannot self-resolve so every case reaches support.

What would fix it: Actionable error messages with reference codes; retry paths; instrument the top failing actions (submit/close/pay/download).

204136204159204100
Hidden Rules

Behaviour: Users discover eligibility rules (off-plan, expired contract, license validity, 5% rent-cap) only after failing.

Why it happens: Business rules are enforced correctly but never surfaced on the service card before users start.

What would fix it: Show pre-conditions and rent-cap logic up-front on the service card; explain why an action is blocked.

204254204137204788
Data Sync

Behaviour: Data updated in TAMM/ELMS/ICP does not propagate to Dari; owned properties/records do not appear.

Why it happens: Cross-system propagation is missing or delayed, so users see stale or conflicting data.

What would fix it: Automated cross-system sync with reconciliation; surface last-synced status; self-serve "record not found" recovery.

204542204847204124
