Recommendations & Opportunities

Prioritised actions linked to the pain points, with the share of tickets each would address.

P0 — Highest volume

Deflect questions with self-serve content & status visibility

Publish service guides, eligibility rules and fee logic in-product; add application-status tracking and proactive notifications so users stop emailing to ask "what's the status".

▸ Targets ~49.1% of tickets (pure questions). 204098204095
P0 — High ROI

Let people update their own information

In-platform identity/profile editor (instant for low-risk fields; verified upload for passport/EID/legal name); company-detail updates; self-serve account creation and application cancellation.

▸ Removes the largest fixable pain (459 tickets). 204790204143204289
P0 — Reliability

Fix & explain failing actions

Instrument the top failing actions (Tawtheeq submit/close, pay, document download); replace generic errors with clear reference codes and retry paths.

▸ Improves 443 error tickets (22.0%). 204136204159204100
P1 — Targeted

Show the rules before users start (incl. the 5% rent cap)

Show pre-conditions (off-plan, contract status, licence validity) and the approved rent-increase logic on the service page before users start; explain why an action is blocked.

▸ Addresses hidden-rule friction and the recurring Tawtheeq 5% rejection. 204137204231204254
P1 — Leverage

Automate data sync across systems

Automated TAMM/ELMS/ICP↔Dari propagation with reconciliation and a last-synced status; self-serve recovery when an owned record is "not found".

▸ Small in volume but cascades into errors and record-not-found. 204542204847204124
