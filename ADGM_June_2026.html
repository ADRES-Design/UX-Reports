1. Executive Summary

A snapshot of the June 2026 helpdesk picture for ADGM: where users get stuck, which services take the heaviest hit, and the highest-impact moves.

How users are getting stuck

Top 5 services by ticket volume

The five things leadership should know

  1. Platform reliability still dominates every other problem. Platform failures — system errors and phantom locks that block users mid-transaction across Lease, Transaction and Certificate — are the single largest category at 42.7% (441 tickets). Authentication failures (login / UAE Pass / record visibility) add a further 7.5% (77). Both grew versus May.
  2. Disclosure gaps are a major, growing pain. Rule, fee and lifecycle confusion is the primary driver for 34.9% of tickets (up from 29.2% in May). Users are increasingly surprised by charges and by automatic lifecycle actions (auto-closure, expiry) that the platform never surfaces at the point of decision.
  3. Users still cannot correct their own data or pull their own reports. No-self-service data fixes (189) and report/extract requests (178) keep support operating as a manual substitute for missing product features.
  4. Multi-party approvals stall silently. Approval breakdowns (283) and POA/PMA friction (154) leave buyers, banks and owners unable to act; 54% of approval tickets also carry an explicit delay.
  5. Delay is the universal amplifier, not a separate issue. 334 tickets (32.4%) cite waiting or chasing, and 96% ride on top of another failure. Cutting resolution time and adding status visibility would lift the felt severity of every theme.
