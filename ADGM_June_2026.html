5. Recurring User Pain Points

Four recurring patterns that explain the frustration — each with the behaviour, why it happens, and the fix.

Transactions fail with no recovery

Behaviour: Users hit errors, blocked fields or non-initiating applications at the moment of value on AccessRP.

Why it happens: The portal is the single point of failure and lacks clear error handling; some breaks follow maintenance and stale license/validation states.

What would fix it: Robust error messages with reference codes; premise-ID/validation fixes; post-maintenance regression testing; AccessRP stability work.

213532135421310
Rules, fees & lifecycle are invisible until users fail

Behaviour: Users discover rent-cap rules, expiry, penalties and fees only after being affected, then ask support to explain.

Why it happens: Business rules are enforced correctly but never surfaced at the point of decision.

What would fix it: Show rules on the service card before users start; proactive expiry/closure notifications; clear fee breakdowns and tax invoices.

213112132421296
Self-service gap forces email support

Behaviour: Routine license/profile edits, document replacement and report pulls are emailed to support.

Why it happens: No in-product editor or export exists, so support becomes the missing UI — and this demand surged in June.

What would fix it: Self-service profile/license editor (instant + verified tracks); document replace; self-service report export.

212882132021298
Approvals & queues stall with no visibility

Behaviour: Users wait on a second party, owner, bank or on ADGM’s own review queue, and chase status repeatedly.

Why it happens: Multi-party steps are manual and status is invisible; internal SLA is not separated from third-party waits.

What would fix it: End-to-end approval tracker; notifications to approvers; publish status; separate internal SLA from third-party waits.

213092131521357

6. Key Trends & Observations

High-level patterns worth surfacing to leadership beyond the headline metrics.

Reliability pressure intensified

System-error prevalence jumped to 52% of coded tickets (multi-label), up from ~33% in May — the single largest code by a wide margin, with premise-ID/validation and post-maintenance breaks recurring.
2135321310

Self-service demand surged

Report-request and data-correction prevalence rose sharply (REPORT-REQ ~10%→24%, DATA-FIX ~19%→31%) — users increasingly use support as the missing UI.
2129821288

Issues recur near-verbatim across months

Kingfield PMA-removal (21308≈21316) and transaction-report requests (21298≈21314) repeat almost word-for-word — and match May patterns — confirming systemic, not one-off, gaps.
2130821298

Rent-cap / 5% increase is an emerging cluster

New inquiries and disputes about the 5% rental-increase cap and large rent hikes emerged in June, spanning knowledge, fee and lifecycle codes.
213112138722000

Demand still concentrates in Lease + Transaction

The two services are ~55% of analysable volume (Lease 35.5% + Transaction 19.6%), so targeted fixes there yield outsized deflection.
2135321310

Madhmoun remains a hotspot but eased

Madhmoun is still error-heavy but the system-error rate fell to 53% (81 of 153) from 69% in May, even as its volume nearly tripled.

8. Recommendations & Opportunities

Prioritised actions linked to the four themes, with the share of tickets addressed.

P0 — Critical

T2 · Harden the platform at the point of transaction

Fix AccessRP registration/renewal and premise-ID validation errors with clear reference codes; add post-maintenance regression testing; resolve stale "expired license" validation states; treat Madhmoun permit-generation and approval-notification failures as a dedicated reliability incident; stabilise login/account-linking.

▸ Addresses system-error + access load — the largest and fastest-growing reliability pressure (~52% multi-label). 213532138121489
P0 — High ROI

T1 · Close the self-service gap (surged in June)

In-platform license activation and profile editing (instant for low-risk fields; verified upload-and-review for nationality/passport/legal-name); document replace; self-service transaction-report and unit-list export.

▸ Removes ~20% of tickets that are pure manual handling; report + data-fix demand rose most sharply MoM. 212882132021298
P1 — High

T3 · Make the rulebook visible in-product

Surface business rules on the service card before users start (auto-closure, expiry, early-termination penalties, the 5% rent-cap); add fee breakdowns + tax-invoice download; contextual help on warnings; proactive lifecycle notifications.

▸ Deflects much of the ~29% knowledge/lifecycle/fee load. 213112132421296
P1 — Targeted

T4 · Orchestrate approvals & expose status

Approval tracker with notifications to second parties/owners/banks; separate internal SLA from third-party waits and publish status; fast-track high-value transfers and mortgage releases; self-service PMA/POA unit management.

▸ Improves ~18% of tickets, high value per case. 213092131521357
