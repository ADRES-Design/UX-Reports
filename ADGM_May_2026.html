The five things leadership should know

  1. The platform breaks at the point of transaction. 44.5% of coded tickets are platform & authentication failures — system errors alone are 37.1% — hitting users at registration, renewal, payment and surrender. Reliability is the single biggest lever. 196221967520456
  2. Rules and fees are invisible until users fail. 29.2% are knowledge, lifecycle and fee tickets — auto-closure, expiry, penalties and charges are enforced correctly but not disclosed upfront. 196592061319653

5. Recurring User Pain Points

Five recurring patterns that explain the frustration — each with the behaviour, why it happens, and the fix.

Transactions fail with no recovery

Behaviour: Users hit errors / blocked fields / won’t-initiate at the moment of value.
Why it happens: The portal is the single point of failure and lacks clear error handling; some breaks follow maintenance.
What would fix it: Robust error messages with reference codes; post-maintenance regression testing; AccessRP stability work.

196221967520456

The Lack of Knowledge & Information

Behaviour: Users discover rules, lifecycle automations and fees only after being affected.
Why it happens: Business rules are enforced but never surfaced at the point of decision.
What would fix it: Show rules on the service card before users start; proactive expiry/closure notifications; clear fee breakdowns.

196592061319653

Self-Service Gap

Behaviour: Routine edits and report pulls are emailed to support.
Why it happens: No in-product editor or export exists, so support becomes the UI.
What would fix it: Self-service profile editor (instant + verified tracks); document replace; report export.

196201965019794

Approval Delays

Behaviour: Users wait on a second party, bank, owner — or on ADGM’s own review queue.
Why it happens: Multi-party steps are manual and status is invisible.
What would fix it: End-to-end approval tracker; notifications to approvers; separate internal-SLA from third-party waits.

196271980919854

7. Recommendations & Opportunities

Prioritised actions linked to the four themes, with quantified impact if implemented.

P0 — Critical

T2 · Harden the platform at the point of transaction

Fix AccessRP registration/renewal errors with clear error codes; add post-maintenance regression testing; treat Madhmoun permit-generation as a dedicated reliability incident; build self-service duplicate/phantom resolution; stabilise UAE Pass / account-linking.

▸ Addresses the largest theme — ~44% of coded tickets.
196221966519760
P0 — High

T3 · Make the rulebook visible in-product

Surface business rules on the service card before users start (auto-closure, expiry, early-termination penalties); add fee breakdowns + tax-invoice download; contextual help on warnings; proactive lifecycle notifications.

▸ Deflects much of the ~29% knowledge/lifecycle/fee load.
196591965319612
P1 — High ROI

T1 · Close the self-service gap

In-platform profile editing (instant for low-risk fields; verified upload-and-review for nationality/passport/legal-name); document replace; self-service report export.

▸ Removes ~14% of tickets that are pure manual handling.
196201971419794
P1 — Targeted

T4 · Orchestrate approvals & expose status

Approval tracker with notifications to second parties/owners; separate internal-SLA from third-party waits and publish status; fast-track high-value transfers and mortgage releases.

▸ Improves ~13% of tickets, high value per case.
