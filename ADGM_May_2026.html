1. Executive Summary

How users are getting stuck

Top 5 services by ticket volume

The five things leadership should know

  1. Platform reliability dominates every other problem. Platform failures — system errors and phantom locks that block users mid-transaction (257 tickets) — are the largest single pain. When the platform breaks, the user is stopped with no recovery path, so this sits underneath most other problems.
  2. Disclosure gaps are a leading pain. Rule, fee and lifecycle confusion sits at 29.3% of tickets — Knowledge & unclear-UI gap (286), Lifecycle & business-rule surprise (226) and Fee & billing dispute. Users are surprised by rules and fees they were never shown up-front.
  3. Users still cannot correct their own data or pull their own reports. No self-service data correction (105) and No self-service reporting (45) keep support busy with routine profile edits, document replacements and report/extract requests that should never become tickets.
  4. Multi-party approvals stall silently. Approval breakdowns (125) and POA / PMA management friction (118) leave buyers, banks and owners unable to act, with no shared view of who is blocking.
  5. Delay is the universal amplifier, not a separate issue. 198 tickets (28%) cite waiting or chasing, and ~90% ride on top of another failure. Cutting resolution time on platform, approval and data-fix issues removes most of the follow-up volume.
